# ARYA site

Next.js 16 on Vercel. Waitlist signups go to Klaviyo list `YxmBfA` via `src/lib/klaviyo-waitlist.ts` (all forms must use `subscribeToKlaviyoWaitlist`).

All copy must follow `.cursor/rules/arya-brand-positioning.mdc` (Decision Log v2.2). Read it before editing any user-facing text.

## Design
Mediterranean limestone, espresso and bronze. Tokens live at the top of `src/app/globals.css`; fonts are Italiana (display) and Josefin Sans (text, the wordmark's face). Logos are in `public/brand/` (from the founders' logo package; espresso-bronze is primary).
Products show `ProductPlaceholder` until real photography exists. Never use AI-generated images of the product.
`StandardCertificate` lists requirements only; never mark a test as passed until it has been run.

## SEO
Target phrase: "premium non-toxic activewear". Always pair "non-toxic" with the standard (OEKO-TEX 100, no added PFAS, no toxic dyes), never with health or science claims.
