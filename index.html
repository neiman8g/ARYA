    About ARYA

    We're Nima and Lucy, a couple in Los Angeles who train together. We spent years paying more for activewear that went sheer, rolled down or wore out, and never knew what was in the fabric. So we started making our own, for him and for her, tailored to real bodies and made with PFAS free, OEKO-TEX® certified fabric. Activewear done right, the way it should have been in the first place.
