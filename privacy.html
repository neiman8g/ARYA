Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you visit arya.clothing or sign up for first access to our first drop, we may collect:

Information you provide: your email address and, if you choose to answer, whether you are shopping for women's or men's pieces. If you email us, we receive your message and email address.

Information collected with your signup: the page address you signed up from and any campaign tags in that link (utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign), so we know which posts and partners bring people to ARYA.

Information collected automatically: like most websites, our hosting provider and the content networks that serve our fonts and scripts receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type and device. We do not run advertising trackers or session recording on this site.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to send you updates about ARYA and the first drop, to understand which channels people find us through, to improve our website, and to respond to your messages.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with service providers we need to run ARYA: Klaviyo for email, Vercel for hosting, and Google Fonts and unpkg for delivering fonts and site code.

3. Emails and unsubscribing

Every email we send includes a link to unsubscribe. You can also ask us to remove you at any time by writing to hello@arya.clothing.

4. Cookies and local storage

This site does not use advertising cookies. During your visit it may keep campaign tags in your browser's session storage so they are not lost if you refresh the page. That storage is cleared when you close the tab. Our email provider may set its own cookies when you open or click our emails.

5. Your rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request that we delete it, to opt out of any sale of personal information (we do not sell data), and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@arya.clothing.

6. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information and work only with established providers. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we do our best to keep your information safe.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at hello@arya.clothing.
