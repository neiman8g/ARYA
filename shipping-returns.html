Shipping and Returns

Last updated October 10, 2026

Ordering

Our collection is not on sale yet. Orders open with the first drop, and people on our email list get first access and founding pricing. Sign up here.

Shipping

Shipping cost: standard shipping within the United States is complimentary on all orders. International shipping details will be shared at launch.

Delivery time: once shipped, domestic orders typically arrive within 5 to 7 business days. We will email you tracking when your order ships.

Returns and exchanges

We want you to love what you wear. Once our products ship, we will offer:

30-day returns: unworn items with tags attached can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

Exchanges: need a different size? We offer free exchanges within 30 days of delivery. Contact us and we will send you the right size.

Condition: items must be unworn, unwashed and in original condition with all tags attached.

How to start a return or exchange

Email hello@arya.clothing with your order number and whether you would like a return or an exchange. We will respond within 1 business day with a prepaid return label.

Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days of receiving the returned item.

Questions

Write to us at hello@arya.clothing. We are happy to help.
